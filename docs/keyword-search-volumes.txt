HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS
-----------------------
- India/mo and US/mo are average monthly Google searches over the last 12 months,
  rounded into Google Ads' usual buckets (10, 20 ... 880, 1,000, 1,300 ... 22,200).
- "<10" means Google reports no meaningful volume. It does not mean zero searches,
  just too few to measure.
- CPC is what advertisers bid per click, as returned by the API (US dollars). A high
  CPC means people searching that term are worth money, i.e. buyer intent.
- "-" means that keyword was not looked up for that country.
- Volumes are for Google only. They do not cover LinkedIn, GitHub or ChatGPT, where a
  lot of recruiter discovery actually happens.


KEY FINDINGS
------------
1. "AI Engineer" is the right headline title.
   India: ai engineer 22,200/mo vs machine learning engineer 4,400/mo (5x).
   US:    ai engineer 12,100/mo vs machine learning engineer  9,900/mo.
   Keep leading with AI Engineer and carry Machine Learning Engineer as the second
   title, as the site already does.

2. Nobody searches for "portfolio" terms or for your name yet.
   "ai engineer portfolio", "machine learning engineer portfolio", "llm developer" and
   "taaran jain" all return <10. Don't spend effort optimising for them. Recruiters reach
   you through LinkedIn, GitHub and your resume link. Search traffic will come from
   your content, not your title.

3. The real opportunity is technical content on the stack you already use.
   langgraph (22,200 US), pgvector (4,400 US), rag pipeline (1,900 US),
   retrieval augmented generation (8,100 India) and agentic ai (49,500 India) are all
   things your projects use. Their competition scores are low (langgraph 0.05,
   rag chunking 0.12, pgvector 0.17), so well-written posts and case studies can rank.

4. Your existing blog post has a clear target.
   "chunking strategies" (320 US) and "rag chunking" (140 US) are realistic for
   /blog/rag-chunking-boundaries. Put "chunking strategies for RAG" in its title, H1
   and description.

5. Freelance terms carry money, mostly in the US.
   hire ai developer 880/mo at $66.95 CPC, ai automation agency 1,900/mo at $20.29,
   ai developers for hire 210/mo at $163.26. CPCs that high mean buyers. These terms
   belong on studio.taaranjain.com; the portfolio should support them with the studio
   link rather than compete for them. The Indian equivalents
   ("freelance ai developer") return <10.

6. Head terms are out of reach for a personal site.
   "data scientist" (165,000 India), "rag" (74,000 US) and "agentic ai" are dominated
   by Wikipedia, IBM, AWS, Coursera and job boards. Use them in copy so Google
   understands the topic, but target the longer phrases below them.
